# Adventure Work Human Resource Department Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Power BI dashboard analysis of the Adventure Work Company's Human Resource and Sales data. The goal is to provide insights into workforce composition, age distribution, and sales performance to support informed decision-making in recruitment, succession planning, and employee performance management.

---

## 🔍 Objectives

- Analyze employee demographics (gender, age, and marital status).
- Evaluate departmental structure and workforce size.
- Identify sales performance by individual salespersons.
- Provide strategic recommendations for HR and Sales departments.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- **Gender Distribution:** 71% of the workforce is male, indicating a significant gender imbalance.
- **Marital Status:** 50.34% of employees are married, and 49.66% are single, suggesting a fairly even distribution.
- **Age Distribution:** The majority of employees fall in the age group of **35–42**, with notable counts in **27–34** and **43–50** ranges.
- **Sales Performance:**
  - **Top Performers:** Linda C. Mitchell ($14M), Jillian Carson ($13M), and Michael G. Blythe ($12M).
  - **Lowest Contributor:** Syed E. Abbas recorded $0 in sales.
- **Departmental Overview:** 6 department groups comprising 16 total departments, with 290 total employees and 17 salespersons.
- **Average Age:** 36 years.

---

## 📌 Recommendations

- **Improve Gender Diversity:** Implement hiring and development programs aimed at increasing female representation in the company.
- **Succession Planning:** With a significant number of employees in the 43–50 and 51–59 age groups, succession plans should be in place for retirement coverage.
- **Sales Mentorship Programs:** Encourage knowledge sharing by pairing high-performing sales staff with those underperforming.
- **Incentivize Performance:** Introduce performance-based bonuses or recognition to motivate mid-tier and lower-tier sales staff.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Power BI:** For creating visual dashboards and data storytelling.
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV:** As a data source for importing HR and sales information.
- **Data Modeling:** Power BI relationships and DAX formulas for dynamic filtering and calculations.
